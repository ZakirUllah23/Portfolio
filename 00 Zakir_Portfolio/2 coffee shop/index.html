Our Story

Born from a passion for exceptional coffee

Founded in 2012, Brew & Bean began as a neighborhood coffee cart with one mission: to serve extraordinary coffee that brings people together. Two friends, a shared love for the craft, and relentless pursuit of quality.

Today we meticulously source our beans from sustainable farms, roast them in small batches, and train our baristas in the art of precision brewing — so every cup that leaves our counter is one we're proud of.

  • Ethically sourced, single-origin beans
  • In-house small-batch roasting
  • Certified Q-grader baristas
  • Compostable packaging & zero-waste practices

Services

More Than Just Coffee

We partner with individuals, businesses, and events to deliver exceptional coffee experiences beyond our four walls.

Delivery & Pickup

Order online for same-day pickup or local delivery within Brooklyn. Fresh coffee to your door in under 45 minutes.

Corporate Catering

Elevate your next meeting, team event, or corporate gathering. We set up, serve, and clean up — you just enjoy.

Bean Subscriptions

Monthly deliveries of freshly roasted, hand-selected beans. Customise your grind size, roast profile, and origin preference.

Brewing Masterclasses

Weekend workshops led by our Q-graders. From V60 to espresso dialling — learn the science and art of specialty coffee.
